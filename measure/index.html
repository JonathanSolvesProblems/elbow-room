    Measure your staircase

    Real inches out of one photograph, in the browser. No upload, no GPU, and no reconstruction: you give it one size it can trust, and it gives you every other size in that plane.

    No site tools in this browser. The measuring works, but the agent cannot help. Use ChatGPT's desktop browser, or Chrome or Edge with chrome://flags/#enable-webmcp-testing enabled.

    How this works

    1. Load a photo of the surface you want to measure.
    2. Click the four corners of something flat, rectangular and lying on that same surface, clockwise from the near left. A stair tread (the flat part you step on), a floor tile, a sheet of paper.
    3. Type its real size below. This is the only thing telling the page what scale the photograph is at, so a guess here is a guess in every number after it.
    4. Now click any two points on that same surface and get real inches.
